Client Protocols

Frequently Addressed Inquiries

We provide express DHL delivery globally. Orders are dispatched from Tokyo or Milan within 24 hours. Delivery takes 2-4 business days for North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. All duties, import taxes, and VAT are fully pre-paid for EU, US, UK, and JP destinations.
NOIRÉ pieces are tailored with deliberate architectural volume, dropped shoulders, and elongated torso dimensions. We suggest ordering your true size to achieve the editorial silhouette envisioned by the designer. If you prefer a more conventional slim fit, choose one size down.
We offer a complimentary 30-day global return and exchange window. Garments must be unworn, unwashed, and in original condition with intact atelier security tags. A prepaid digital DHL return label is included with every digital invoice.
Due to our high-density organic cottons (320 to 520 GSM) and mineral pigment dye treatments, we recommend cold delicate washing inside-out with gentle liquid detergent. Always hang dry in indirect shade. Never tumble dry, as extreme machine heat degrades natural loopback terry loft.
